## 正體中文（zh-TW）

### 說明

HeiBan 黑板是一款 Windows 白板與筆記工具，適合教學、簡報、整理想法與日常手寫。依照你的工作方式，選擇多頁筆記、無邊界畫布，或有固定尺寸的黑板，將文字、筆跡、圖形與圖片整理在同一份作品中。

使用畫筆、螢光筆、直線、箭頭與各種形狀表達想法，加入文字或便條紙補充重點。透過物件圖層、調色盤與屬性面板，調整顏色、外觀及排列；尺規、圓規、雷射、計算機與計時器也能協助教學和展示。

開啟螢幕畫布，即可在桌面與其他應用程式上方繪製註記，方便講解畫面與標示重點。工具列及面板可依使用習慣調整位置，畫布背景也可選擇橫線、點狀、方格或五線譜。

作品保存在本機，日常繪圖與使用說明可離線使用。支援可編輯作品檔，以及 PNG、SVG、PDF、HTML 等匯出方式，方便保存與分享。提供正體中文等十種介面語言及對應使用說明。

## English（en）

### 說明

HeiBan is a whiteboard and note-taking app for Windows, designed for teaching, presentations, brainstorming and everyday handwriting. Choose a multi-page notebook, an infinite canvas or a board with a fixed size, and bring handwriting, text, shapes and images together in one work.

Express ideas with pens, highlighters, lines, arrows and shapes. Add text and sticky notes, then arrange and style your objects using layers, a color palette and the properties panel. A ruler, compass, laser pointer, calculator and timer help you explain and present your ideas.

Screen Canvas lets you draw annotations over your desktop and other applications. Position toolbars and panels to suit your workflow, and choose plain, ruled, dotted, grid or music staff backgrounds.

Your works are stored locally. Everyday drawing and the user guide are available offline. Save editable works or export to PNG, SVG, PDF and HTML for sharing. The interface and offline guides are available in ten languages.

### 此版本的新增功能

- Added ten interface languages, selectable from the home screen. Traditional Chinese is the default.
- Added offline guides in ten languages, with search, chapter navigation and a reading language selector.
- Added an optional auto-save setting, disabled by default. Save manually or when leaving a work or exiting normally; a progress animation appears while saving on exit.
- Improved opening and selecting works, canvas background options and cursor feedback when adjusting shapes.
- Improved the dedicated layers panel and drag position feedback, with more focused object properties, arrow size controls, view pin colors and sticky note styles.
- Added layers and a color palette to Screen Canvas, restored the eraser range preview and improved toolbar sizing and layout.

### 產品功能

- Multi-page notebooks, an infinite canvas and fixed-size boards for different workflows.
- Editable pen strokes and highlighters; hold a nearly straight stroke briefly to straighten it.
- Lines, arrows, geometric shapes and curves, with appearance and arrow size controls.
- Text formatting and multiple sticky note styles for notes and highlights.
- Transparent Screen Canvas for drawing over the desktop and other applications.
- Ruler, compass, laser pointer, calculator and timer for teaching and presentations.
- Dedicated layers panel with drag reordering, renaming, hiding and locking.
- Select, multi-select, move, resize and rotate objects, with undo and redo.
- Color palette and object properties for strokes, fills and opacity.
- Import images, paste screenshots and crop images to combine visual materials.
- Plain, ruled, dotted, grid and music staff canvas backgrounds.
- Work library with search, format filters, favorites and trash recovery.
- Optional auto-save, disabled by default, plus manual saving and saving on normal exit.
- Export editable .heiban/JSON works, as well as PNG, SVG, PDF and HTML.
- Dock toolbars and panels in different positions and customize toolbar items.
- Ten interface languages and matching offline guides; Traditional Chinese is the default.
